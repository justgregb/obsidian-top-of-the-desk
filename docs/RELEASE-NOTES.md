# Top of the Desk 0.9.50

Top of the Desk keeps your current work within reach.

Bring the things you’re working on together in Obsidian: a draft, a reading list, today’s note, and the references you keep returning to.

Add notes, folders, Bases, canvases, PDFs, audio, images, and websites. Arrange them your way, group related notes into stacks, and keep a scratchpad close for passing thoughts. Everything stays in its original place in your vault.

Return to recently opened items, continue your daily writing, and see your writing activity—all from your desk.

The package includes an optional demo vault and five screenshots captured with fictional sample content. Runtime release files are `main.js`, `manifest.json`, and `styles.css`.

This is a prerelease, distributed under the MIT License. The production build, version consistency, packaged asset checksums, documentation/image links, and included demo screenshots have been checked. The JavaScript executable content matches the supplied 0.9.50 runtime; its third-party notice is now included in the bundle.

The manifest retains its declared Obsidian 1.11.0 minimum and mobile eligibility. The supplied screenshots cover macOS with Obsidian 1.14.4. Automated regression tests, typechecking, minimum-version compatibility, mobile/touch behavior, and broader runtime checks have not been run for this release.

For manual installation, place `main.js`, `manifest.json`, and `styles.css` in `.obsidian/plugins/top-of-the-desk/` in your vault and enable the plugin. When updating, keep your existing `data.json`. The plugin is not yet listed in the Community directory.
