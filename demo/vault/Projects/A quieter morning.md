# A quieter morning

The best mornings leave a little room around the edges. Before the messages and the meetings, there is time to notice the light moving across the kitchen table.

I want to start the day with something small and deliberate: a page of notes, a short walk, a question I do not need to answer yet.

## What I am trying

- Leave the phone in another room until breakfast.
- Write one paragraph before opening the inbox.
- Keep a place for the interesting things that do not fit the plan.

The point is not to make a perfect routine. It is to make enough space to pay attention.
